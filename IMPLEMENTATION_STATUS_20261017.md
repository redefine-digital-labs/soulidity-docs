Recorded October 17, 2026 · Companion to the [English whitepaper](WHITEPAPER.md).

## Scope and interpretation

The whitepaper defines the target. This dated record distinguishes available evidence from unfinished work. Source presence, local tests, independent acceptance, source cutover, publication, deployment and adoption are separate.

The full engineering scope retains ten modules and fifteen maintenance units, the original 157 records, 30 responsibility groups and 1,528 production-review identities. Repository counts and passing local checks do not establish completion of those units.

## Source and remote reconciliation

GitHub readback on October 17 confirmed [Animacraft PR82](https://github.com/redefine-digital-labs/animacraft/pull/82) and [Soulidity PR34](https://github.com/redefine-digital-labs/soulidity/pull/34) are merged. Historical worktree text describing them as awaiting merge does not override that state. Merge does not establish current deployment, compatible new-protocol configuration or end-to-end acceptance.

Incoming snapshots contain earlier source and historical paths alongside newer product worktrees. Differences from current local owning sources require comparison; they are not permission to overwrite later accepted changes or replay old cutover scripts.

## Current engineering priority

Prove direct Soul creation or receipt of an existing compatible Soul → content and permission management → an actual supported use → cold continuation and compatible-client takeover. This lifecycle must not require Animacraft, a Maker, a channel or a market.

Maker/Pack authoring, publication and Complete, visual composition and Loadouts, trading and buyer continuation, Channel, Community, Physical, SoulDesk, Engine, the games, documentation and third-party integration remain in scope. Every segment must support appropriate independent entry and continuation.

## Remaining requirements

- Actual UI/SDK/type/schema pairing with canonical producer and consumer contracts, followed by normal, permission, funds, cancellation and recovery verification.
- Complete protected-content roles, necessary Blob availability and renewal, signed/effects recovery, supported custody and relevant old-state migration evidence.
- Qualified independent acceptance and exact release, deployment, monitoring, reconciliation and recovery records.
- External developer and user evidence; internal examples and simulation do not establish adoption.

Consistent old-chain databases/genesis and browser draft/session recovery exports are not supplied by the public source transfer. Their absence remains relevant to old-state restoration; it does not prevent unrelated new-target local development.

## Current policies

Owned Account remains the selected canonical policy. A historical shared-Account proposal is not adopted. Nonzero nested-child royalty allocation is explicitly unsupported in this version. Rebuilt A/B are valid new local baselines; locating historical Makers or goldens is not a prerequisite for that work.

V1 retains the no-protocol-token baseline. Future networks and token proposals are separate directions, not released capabilities or launch commitments.

## Documentation publication boundary

The English whitepaper v0.3, journey/composability companion, references and review record are documentation deliverables. This revision does not rerun product tests or change the counts above. Engineering can advance independently after the observations recorded here. Publishing documentation does not deploy software, execute asset transactions or establish protocol acceptance.
